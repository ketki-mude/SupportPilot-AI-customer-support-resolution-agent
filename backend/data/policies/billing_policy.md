# Billing policy

This policy explains how and when Acme charges your payment method, and how billing problems are handled.

## 1. Payment methods

### 1.1 Accepted methods
Acme accepts major credit and debit cards. Your card is identified in our records and in support conversations only by its last four digits.

### 1.2 Card security
Acme support will never ask for your full card number, security code, or password. Do not share them in chat. If someone asks for them, contact us.

## 2. When you are charged

### 2.1 Authorization and capture
When you place an order we place a temporary hold, called an authorization, for the order total. The charge is captured either when the order is placed or when it ships, depending on the item.

### 2.2 Released holds
If an order is cancelled before the charge is captured, the hold is released and no money is taken. Your bank may take 3 to 5 business days to remove a released hold from your statement.

## 3. Refunds to your payment method

### 3.1 Original payment method
All refunds go back to the card used for the order. We cannot move a refund to a different card.

### 3.2 Refund timing
Refunds are sent as soon as they are approved. Most banks post the credit within 5 to 10 business days.

## 4. Billing problems

### 4.1 Duplicate or unrecognised charges
If you see a charge twice or a charge you do not recognise, contact support. Billing disputes are reviewed by our billing team, who will follow up with you directly.

### 4.2 Incorrect amounts
If you were charged a different amount than the order total, tell us the order number. A specialist will compare the charge with your order record.

## 5. Currency

### 5.1 Prices and charges
All prices, charges, and refunds are in US dollars.
