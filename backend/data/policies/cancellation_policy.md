# Cancellation policy

This policy explains when an Acme order can be cancelled and what happens to your payment.

## 1. Before the order ships

### 1.1 Cancelling a processing order
Orders that are still processing can be cancelled at any time, whatever their value. Cancellation takes effect immediately and the order will not ship.

### 1.2 What happens to your payment
If you were already charged, the full amount is refunded to your original payment method in the same step. If the charge was only authorized, the hold is released instead and no money is taken. See the billing policy for timing.

## 2. After the order ships

### 2.1 Shipped and in transit orders
Once an order has shipped it can no longer be cancelled automatically. A support specialist will review the request and may be able to stop the delivery or arrange a return when it arrives.

## 3. After delivery

### 3.1 Delivered orders
Delivered orders cannot be cancelled. You can still request a refund or replacement under the refund policy within 30 days of delivery.

## 4. Orders that are already cancelled

### 4.1 Cancelled orders
If an order is already cancelled, no further action is needed. Any refund or released hold from the cancellation follows the timing in the billing policy.
