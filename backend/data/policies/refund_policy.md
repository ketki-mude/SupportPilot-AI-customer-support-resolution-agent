# Refund policy

This policy explains when Acme refunds an order or ships a replacement, and how to ask for either.

## 1. Who this policy covers

### 1.1 Eligible customers
Refunds and replacements are available to the customer who placed and paid for the order. We can only discuss or change an order with the account holder who owns it. Requests about someone else's order are declined, even if the order number is correct.

### 1.2 How to make a request
Contact Acme support through chat with your order number (for example ORD-10001) and a short description of the problem. Photos help for damaged items but are not required to start a request.

## 2. Eligibility

### 2.1 Delivered orders only
Refunds and replacements apply to orders that have been delivered. If your order is still processing, shipped, or in transit, we will share its current status and expected delivery date instead. If an order is late and has passed its estimated delivery date, a support specialist will investigate with the carrier.

### 2.2 Refund window
You can request a refund or replacement within 30 days of the delivery date. The day of delivery counts as day 0, so a request on day 30 is still accepted and a request on day 31 is not. Delivery dates are taken from the carrier's tracking record.

### 2.3 One resolution per order
Each order can receive one resolution: either a refund or a replacement, once. If an order has already been refunded, fully or partly, or a replacement has already been sent, we cannot issue another refund or replacement for it.

## 3. Common reasons for a refund

### 3.1 Change of mind
Unused items in their original packaging can be refunded within the refund window. Return shipping for change-of-mind returns is not covered.

### 3.2 Damaged or defective items
If an item arrives damaged, broken, or does not work, you can choose a full refund of the item price or a free replacement of the same item. Tell us which item is affected. You do not need to return a damaged item unless we ask you to.

### 3.3 Wrong item received
If we sent the wrong item, we will refund it or send the correct item at no cost. We may email a prepaid label so you can return the wrong item.

### 3.4 Item marked delivered but not received
If tracking shows an order as delivered but it never reached you, check with neighbours and your building's mail area first. If it still cannot be found, contact us within 30 days of the delivery date. These requests may be reviewed by a specialist before a refund or replacement is issued.

## 4. Refund amounts and timing

### 4.1 How much is refunded
We refund the price you paid for the affected items. The amount is calculated from your order record, not from the request, so it always matches what was charged.

### 4.2 Where the money goes
Refunds go back to the original payment method, shown by the last four digits of the card. We cannot send a refund to a different card or account.

### 4.3 How long it takes
Refunds are issued straight away once approved. Most banks show the credit within 5 to 10 business days.

## 5. Reviews and approvals

### 5.1 Requests reviewed by our team
Some requests are reviewed by a member of our support team before they are processed, for example higher-value orders. A review usually finishes within one business day, and we will update you in the same conversation.

### 5.2 Requests handled by a specialist
Very high-value orders, late deliveries, and accounts that need extra verification are handled by a specialist rather than processed automatically. A specialist will follow up with you directly.

## 6. Replacements

### 6.1 Choosing a replacement
Instead of a refund you can ask for a replacement of the same item. Replacements follow the same eligibility rules as refunds, including the 30-day window and one resolution per order.

### 6.2 Replacement shipping
Replacements ship at no cost to you, usually within two business days, using the same delivery address as the original order.
