# Shipping policy

This policy covers how Acme processes, ships, and tracks orders, and what to do if a delivery goes wrong.

## 1. Order processing

### 1.1 Processing time
New orders are prepared within 1 to 2 business days. While an order is processing it has not left our warehouse yet, and it can still be cancelled.

### 1.2 Order statuses
An order moves through these statuses: processing, shipped, in transit, and delivered. Shipped means the carrier has collected the parcel. In transit means it is on its way through the carrier's network.

## 2. Delivery and tracking

### 2.1 Tracking numbers
Every shipped order has a tracking number from the carrier. You can ask support for the latest status at any time.

### 2.2 Estimated delivery dates
Each shipped order has an estimated delivery date. Most orders arrive within 3 to 5 business days of shipping. Estimates can move by a day or two during busy periods or bad weather.

### 2.3 Delivery address
The delivery address cannot be changed once an order has shipped. Before shipping, contact support to update it.

## 3. Late and lost packages

### 3.1 Late deliveries
If an order is still in transit after its estimated delivery date, contact support. A specialist will open an investigation with the carrier and keep you updated. If the carrier confirms the parcel is lost, we will offer a refund or a replacement.

### 3.2 Marked delivered but missing
If tracking says delivered but the parcel is not there, see section 3.4 of the refund policy.

## 4. Damage in transit

### 4.1 Reporting damage
If a parcel or its contents arrive damaged, report it within 30 days of delivery. Damaged items are covered by section 3.2 of the refund policy.

## 5. Shipping costs

### 5.1 Standard shipping
Standard shipping is included in the order total. Replacement shipments are always free.
